FAQ

IT staffing questions

What IT staffing solutions does PrimeSupportCo offer?

Contract IT staffing, contract-to-hire solutions, and direct-hire recruitment, along with candidate sourcing and screening across core IT disciplines.

How does technical recruiting work?

We begin with role requirements and team context, then source and screen candidates against technical and communication needs before presenting qualified professionals to hiring managers.

Can staffing complement managed IT services?

Yes. Some organizations need both ongoing managed IT support and additional technical talent. Staffing and managed services can work together when capacity or specialized skills are required.
