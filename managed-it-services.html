FAQ

Managed IT services questions

What are managed IT services?

Managed IT services are recurring technology support and operations delivered by a managed service provider. They typically include helpdesk support, device and endpoint management, infrastructure maintenance, security, and ongoing administration—rather than one-off repair visits.

How is managed IT different from break-fix support?

Break-fix support responds after something fails. Managed IT combines responsive support with proactive maintenance, patching, backups, and structured incident handling as an ongoing partnership.

Who is this service for?

Organizations and IT teams that need dependable business IT support, clear ownership of day-to-day technology operations, and a partner that can work alongside internal staff or serve as the primary support function.

Do you only provide remote support?

We provide remote helpdesk support and can support on-site needs as part of managed IT delivery when the engagement requires it. Coverage details are defined during consultation.
